
#### EDULEVY PROJECT ####

EduLevy is a school fee management system for institutions that collect fees from parents, including primary schools, high schools, and training organisations. Today a parent has to travel to a bank branch, queue to pay, collect a printed receipt, and carry it to the school office as proof of payment. EduLevy lets them pay from their phone instead, without needing internet or a smartphone, and receive a receipt on the same handset. The moment a payment completes, the school's accountant sees it appear on their dashboard with the student's name and admission number, and the student's status updates automatically. Several schools can use the system at once, and no school can see another school's records.
